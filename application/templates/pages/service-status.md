**The Planning Data Platform has been experiencing technical issues.**
Check this page for further updates.

## Updates

### 23 November 2026

Planning authorities can now use a temporary process to [provide data](https://provide.planning.data.gov.uk/) to the Planning Data Platform, including to [publish plan timetable data](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/publish-your-plan-data/publish-your-plan-data) and [other datasets](https://www.planning.data.gov.uk/guidance/).

The temporary process will be available until MHCLG has completed improvements to the Check and Provide service.

Planning authorities can update datasets they are already providing by making changes at the endpoint URL.

We cannot confirm an exact date when the service will be available again. We estimate this will be before the year’s end.

### 16 November 2026

Public access to the **Planning Data Platform**, including the **Planning Data API** and navigation to **Extract,** has been restored with some minor changes.

However, some services including Check and Provide will take longer to reappear. This may affect planning authorities who are:

- [Publishing your plan data](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/publish-your-plan-data/publish-your-plan-data)
- [Publishing other datasets](https://www.planning.data.gov.uk/guidance/), including those supported by Extract and the Digital Planning Improvement Fund

#### Advice for Planning Data API users

Some users may need to review the [Changelog](https://www.planning.data.gov.uk/docs) provided in documentation to resume using the API.

#### Advice for Check and Provide service users

MHCLG is working on improvements to the Check and Provide service.

At this stage we cannot confirm an exact date when the service will reappear. We estimate this will be before the year’s end.

Users who are publishing plan timetable datasets:

- Planning authorities must still follow the [approved data standard](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/publish-your-plan-data/publish-your-plan-data#approved-data-standard) to publish your plan dataset and your plan timetable dataset in accordance with the Planning Data (England) Regulations 2026.
- Guidance on publishing your plan data is available on [Create or Update a Local Plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/publish-your-plan-data/publish-your-plan-data). You are still able to comply with the data standard; however, you will be unable to complete some of the optional steps suggested until the service returns.
- Please contact [digitalland@communities.gov.uk](mailto:digitalland@communities.gov.uk) if you have any questions about how to deliver on your responsibilities to publish your plan data.

Users who are publishing other datasets:

- Planning authorities are encouraged to continue standardising supported datasets in accordance with [guidance on the Planning Data Platform](https://www.planning.data.gov.uk/guidance/).
- [Extract](https://extract.ai.gov.uk/) can still be accessed directly. However, it will not be possible to check and provide it until the service returns.
- MHCLG’s Digital Planning Programme will provide further advice about alternative ways to publish planning datasets on the Planning Data Platform as soon as possible.

### 11 November 2026

We expect to restore public access to the Planning Data Platform including the Planning Data API and navigation to [Extract](https://extract.ai.gov.uk/) to minimise continued disruption as soon as possible.

However, some services including [Check and Provide](https://provide.planning.data.gov.uk/) and [Submit a site for a local plan](https://mhclgdigital.blog.gov.uk/2026/10/06/a-faster-start-to-local-plan-making-introducing-the-submit-a-site-for-a-local-plan-tool/) will take longer to reappear.

### 9 November 2026

The disruption may impact MHCLG services including:

- [Planning Data Platform](https://www.planning.data.gov.uk/)
- Planning Data API, and any third party software services using this data
- [Check and Provide](https://provide.planning.data.gov.uk/)
- [Planning Data Design](https://design.planning.data.gov.uk/)
- [Submit a site for a local plan](https://mhclgdigital.blog.gov.uk/2026/10/06/a-faster-start-to-local-plan-making-introducing-the-submit-a-site-for-a-local-plan-tool/)
- Users of [Extract](https://extract.ai.gov.uk/) can still access the tool directly.

MHCLG’s Digital Planning Programme are working hard to restore services as soon as possible.

### 6 November 2026

Planning Data Platform was taken offline.
